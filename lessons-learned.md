# Lessons Learned – Banking Application Modernization

## 1. Project Overview

This document summarizes lessons learned from a hypothetical banking application modernization project.

**Project type:** Banking application modernization  
**Delivery approach:** Hybrid Agile  
**Project status:** Illustrative case study  
**Purpose:** Document potential challenges, corrective actions, and recommendations for future IT projects.

All scenarios and examples in this document are fictional and created for portfolio demonstration purposes.

## 2. What Went Well

| Area | Observation | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Project governance | Regular status meetings and clearly defined owners | Improved accountability and visibility |
| Requirements | Requirements were documented and reviewed with stakeholders | Reduced ambiguity during development |
| Risk management | Risks and issues were tracked in a centralized RAID log | Enabled earlier escalation |
| Testing | SIT and UAT planning began before release | Improved readiness for deployment |
| Communication | Weekly stakeholder updates were maintained | Supported timely decisions |

## 3. Challenges and Corrective Actions

| Challenge | Potential Impact | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|
| Changing business requirements | Scope and schedule changes | Introduce formal change control and impact assessment |
| Legacy system dependencies | Delays in integration and testing | Maintain a dependency tracker and engage technical owners early |
| Delayed test data availability | Reduced testing time | Confirm test data ownership and readiness before SIT |
| Limited stakeholder availability | Delayed approvals and decisions | Establish decision owners and escalation timelines |
| Defects identified near release | Increased deployment risk | Introduce defect triage and release-readiness checkpoints |

## 4. Key Lessons Learned

### Lesson 1: Confirm requirements early

Document business requirements, acceptance criteria, assumptions, and dependencies before development begins.

### Lesson 2: Manage dependencies proactively

Maintain a dependency register with an owner, target date, status, and escalation path for each critical dependency.

### Lesson 3: Treat testing as a workstream

Develop the SIT and UAT strategy during planning. Define entry and exit criteria, test ownership, defect severity, and sign-off requirements.

### Lesson 4: Maintain clear governance

Use a consistent reporting cadence, RAID log, decision log, and change-control process to keep stakeholders aligned.

### Lesson 5: Plan deployment and support

Prepare a cutover checklist, rollback approach, business communication plan, and hypercare support model before production deployment.

## 5. Recommendations for Future Projects

- Conduct stakeholder and system dependency assessments during initiation.
- Establish a baseline schedule with milestones and accountable owners.
- Review risks, issues, actions, and dependencies at regular intervals.
- Track requirements through development, testing, and business sign-off.
- Define go/no-go criteria and obtain formal release approval.
- Capture lessons learned throughout delivery rather than only at project closure.

## 6. Project Manager's Reflection

Effective IT project delivery requires coordination across business stakeholders, engineering, QA, operations, and vendors. A structured approach to requirements, governance, risk management, testing, and release readiness helps improve transparency and supports informed project decisions.
