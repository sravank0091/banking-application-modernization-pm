4. Schedule and Dependency Management

Maintain an integrated project schedule.

Track milestones, task owners, and dependencies.

Review critical path activities weekly.

Escalate schedule variances and resource constraints.

Document approved changes to the baseline.

5. Change Management

Record change requests.

Assess scope, schedule, cost, and risk impacts.

Obtain approval from authorized stakeholders.

Update project plans and communicate changes.

Track implementation through closure.

6. Communication and Reporting

Weekly status reports will include:

Overall project status

Completed and upcoming milestones

Schedule and scope variances

Key risks, issues, and dependencies

Decisions and stakeholder actions required

7. Quality and Acceptance

Requirements must have documented acceptance criteria.

SIT validates application and system integrations.

UAT validates business requirements.

Critical defects must be addressed or formally dispositioned before release.

Business acceptance and release approvals must be documented.
