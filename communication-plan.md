
# Stakeholder Communication Plan

## Banking Application Modernization

**Project:** Fictional Banking Application Modernization  
**Role:** IT Project Manager (Illustrative)  
**Methodology:** Hybrid Agile

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how project information is communicated to stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

The objective is to ensure timely updates, clear accountability, effective escalation, and alignment across business and technology teams.

## 2. Stakeholder Communication Matrix

| Stakeholder | Information Required | Frequency | Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project Sponsor | Overall health, budget, major risks, decisions | Monthly | Steering committee |
| Business Owners | Requirements, scope, UAT, business sign-off | Weekly | Status meeting |
| Development Team | Sprint progress, blockers, dependencies | Daily | Stand-up |
| QA Team | Test progress, defects, testing blockers | Weekly | Test review |
| Infrastructure Team | Environment readiness, deployment dependencies | Weekly | Technical meeting |
| Operations Team | Support readiness, cutover, handover | Before release | Readiness review |

## 3. Escalation Process

1. Identify and document the issue or risk.
2. Assign an accountable owner and target resolution date.
3. Assess the impact on scope, schedule, cost, or quality.
4. Escalate unresolved critical items to the project sponsor.
5. Record decisions and track actions to closure.

## 4. Reporting Standards

Weekly project status reports will include:

- Overall project health
- Completed and upcoming milestones
- Schedule and scope changes
- Key risks, issues, and dependencies
- Decisions required from stakeholders
- Action owners and due dates

## 5. Governance Principles

- Communicate risks early and transparently.
- Maintain a single source of truth for project status.
- Document decisions and approvals.
- Escalate material changes through the agreed governance process.
- Protect confidential business and customer information.
